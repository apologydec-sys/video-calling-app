My Development Journey

From curiosity to building systems

My journey started with simple curiosity. I wanted to understand how computers and applications worked, so I began experimenting and learning programming. With time, I moved from simply using software to actually building my own systems.

Every project has taught me something new. Some projects worked immediately, while others came with difficult errors and challenges. Instead of giving up, I used those problems as opportunities to learn more about programming, operating systems, databases, servers, and deployment.

Today, I continue improving my skills by building real-world projects and learning new technologies. I am especially interested in creating useful systems that solve real problems and can be used by people in everyday life.
